# Provider landscape and achievable integration depth

**High-confidence recommendation:** integrate by capability, not logo. A service may support catalog search and commercial links but no user-library API or native playback. A retailer may sell a title that another service fulfills. An open API may still require a commercial license.

Priority: **P1** first useful tranche, **P2** follow-on or approved partner work, **P3** exploratory/low-priority. Priority is a product recommendation, not a statement that access has been granted. Scope codes below describe the proposed integration, not shipped functionality.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| C | Catalog, metadata, availability or offers |
| A | Supported user-account/library access |
| L | Loans, holds or other approved circulation |
| N | Native playback/reading of authorized files or streams through existing engines |
| S | Official provider SDK/embed, with its own playback restrictions |
| F | User-directed import of a legitimately supplied compatible file |
| H | Open official app/site; no internal entitlement/progress claim |
| M | Merchant/affiliate offer, after approval for the exact distribution surface |

Confidence is explicit per row. **Unknown** means the required commercial/access/technical contract was not verified. The extended candidate lists deliberately identify what to investigate; they do not advertise implemented support.

Current code handoffs are limited to curated official destinations in `ExternalAccessDirectory`. Excluded from the directory and availability surface: Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+. Amazon's book, Kindle, music and comics storefront routes are separate retail/music products and carry no Prime Video behavior.

| Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night | Current v4 title/ID and country/service/offer routes, episode links where supplied. [S32](SOURCES.md#s32) | Published commercial-use/attribution terms; confirm current plan limits and artwork rights. | P1 benchmark; high documented route |
| TMDB watch providers | Country/provider availability plus provided TMDB watch URL. [S06](SOURCES.md#s06) | JustWatch attribution required; no full provider deep links. Monetized use needs commercial licensing. | Useful fallback if license fits; not a free substitute for a contracted deep-link feed |
| Reelgood business data / Gracenote / other licensed EPG suppliers | Procurement alternative for missing regions, sports or TV guide data. | No quote/access/API tested in this audit. | P3 fallback procurement; unknown cost |

Bake-off: 60 manually reviewed titles (20 Canadian mainstream, 10 French Canadian, 10 anime, 10 film/rental edge cases, 10 episode/edition cases), two regions including CA, and a separate 20-event sports sample if sports is in scope. These are proposed samples, not completed experiments. Do not report a coverage percentage until the sample is run.
