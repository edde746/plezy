Upstream source: https://github.com/moonlight-stream/moonlight-android
PlezyNG adapter and reproducible source preparation:
https://github.com/snapetech-org/PlezyNG/tree/main/android/moonlight
https://github.com/snapetech-org/PlezyNG/blob/main/scripts/games/prepare_moonlight.py

PlezyNG modifications make the engine app-private, add pending-request
cancellation/deadlines, and connect pairing, libraries and touch controls to the
PlezyNG interface. The application ID remains org.snapetech.plezyng.
